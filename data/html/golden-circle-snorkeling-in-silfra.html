Tour Description

This 10-hour tour combines the major attractions of the Golden Circle with a guided snorkeling session in the Silfra Fissure.

Join this easy, full-day tour from Reykjavík for a combination of Iceland’s famous geology and a unique aquatic adventure, available all year round.

The day focuses on the essential Golden Circle route. It begins at Þingvellir National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its exposed rift valley and historical significance as the site of Iceland's first parliament. Here, the unique snorkeling experience takes place in the Silfra Fissure, which is filled with exceptionally clear, filtered glacier meltwater, offering visibility over 100 meters. A certified PADI Dive Master provides safety briefings and equips you with a full drysuit, thermal undersuit, and other gear, ensuring warmth and comfort despite the cold water (2°C–4°C). You will literally float between the tectonic continents.

Following the snorkeling activity, the tour continues to the Geysir Geothermal Area to witness the reliable eruptions of the Strokkur geyser, shooting water up to 35 meters high. The final stop is Gullfoss, the "Golden Waterfall," where the powerful glacial river dramatically plunges into a deep canyon.

The tour includes round-trip transportation from Reykjavík, all snorkeling gear, hot chocolate, and complimentary GoPro underwater photos. (Note: Participants must be able to swim, and specific age/health requirements apply for snorkeling.)

Map and Itinerary

Day tour: Golden Circle & Snorkeling in Silfra

This unforgettable small-group tour takes you to the iconic sights of the Golden Circle, including the historic Þingvellir National Park, the powerful Gullfoss waterfall, and the erupting Geysir geothermal area. The unique highlight of this trip is the chance to snorkel in the Silfra Fissure, swimming between the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates in water renowned for being the clearest in the world.

Please note: The itinerary is subject to change due to weather, road conditions, and availability of activities. Your guide will always ensure the best and safest experience possible.

Your adventure starts 8:00 AM in Reykjavík; after meeting your group, you will begin a comfortable minibus journey through the Golden Circle, complete with local stories and photo tips from your guide.

Please note: We advise our participants to be ready at their designated pickup location at 8:00 am and wait for the bus to arrive around 8:05am till 8:45am depending on road and weather conditions.

Thingvellir National Park

Þingvellir National Park is an extraordinary place where you can see the visible gap created by the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates as they drift apart—a massive geologic event rarely seen so clearly anywhere else. In addition to its stunning geology, Þingvellir boasts Iceland's largest natural lake, which supports diverse fish and bird life, earning it the nickname "The Galapagos of the North."

The park's significance is also deeply historical and cultural. Its name, which translates to "Parliament Plains," comes from the fact that it was the original location of Iceland's parliament (the Althing) when it was founded in 930 AD. This makes Þingvellir a must-see for anyone interested in Icelandic heritage.

Geysir Geothermal Area

The Geysir Geothermal Area is a stunning location full of powerful hot springs. Its star attraction is the Strokkur geyser, which reliably erupts a spectacular column of water up to 35 meters high every 5–10 minutes.

The name for all erupting hot springs—geyser—actually originates from this very location! It was inspired by the area's oldest and now dormant spring, simply named Geysir.

Gullfoss Waterfall

Your journey concludes at one of Iceland's most magnificent sights: Gullfoss, the "Golden Waterfall." This awe-inspiring waterfall is created by the melting water of Langjökull, Iceland's second-largest glacier, which dramatically cascades into a deep canyon.

On sunny days, the dense mist rising from the falls catches the light, often creating a stunning rainbow—making it the ideal spot for breathtaking photos. You can even glimpse the massive Langjökull Glacier in the distance!

Mandatory Snorkeling Requirements and Conditions

To ensure your safety and comfort during this snorkeling tour, please note the following strict requirements:

  • Medical Statement: Participants MUST read and agree to the Snorkeling Medical Statement – click here. Please send out the signed document to snorkeling@troll.is after booking. Guides will deny participation if requirements are not met.
  • Physical & Communication: You must be able to swim, be physically fit, and be able to communicate and understand in English for safety instructions. Pregnant individuals are not permitted.
  • Age and Size Limits: The minimum age is 12 years old, and the maximum age is 69 (ages 60-69 require a signed doctor's medical waiver). Participants must be between 135 cm and 200 cm in height and 30 kg and 120 kg in weight.
  • For children under the age of 12, exceptions to the minimum age requirement may occasionally be made. If you wish to inquire about an exception, please email us at info@troll.is with the child's age, weight, and height so we can assess their suitability based on necessary size and safety requirements.
  • Dry Suit Use: You must be prepared to wear a dry suit (tight at the neck) to protect against cold water; please note that slight water leakage around the neck and sleeves area may happen, and that is considered normal.
  • Minors: Participants under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal adult.
  • Summer Advice: Due to high seasonal demand, booking an earliest morning or late afternoon tour is recommended for the best experience.
