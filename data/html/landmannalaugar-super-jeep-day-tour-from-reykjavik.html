Tour Description

Explore the rugged southern Icelandic highlands on this full-day tour from Reykjavík. This journey takes you off the paved main roads and into a remote landscape shaped by volcanic activity.

Your first stop is Hjálparfoss, a unique twin waterfall framed by distinct basalt rock structures. From here, the route travels inland along rough gravel roads into the Þjórsárdalur Valley. You will visit Háifoss, one of the tallest waterfalls in Iceland, which drops 122 meters (400 feet) into a deep river canyon. Right next to it is a second waterfall called Granni, which means "neighbor" in Icelandic. Pop culture fans will recognize this panoramic canyon edge from the final episode of the television series Stranger Things. In reality, the production crew traveled to this exact spot to film the finale scene with actress Millie Bobby Brown, though the town in the background was added digitally.

Next, the tour stops at Hnausapollur, a large volcanic crater lake known for its deep blue water and steep rims. The final destination is Landmannalaugar, a famous geothermal area located roughly 180 kilometers (111 miles) from the capital. Your guide will lead you on a scenic hike past steaming vents, lava fields, and multicolored rhyolite mountains. After the hike, you will have time to bathe in a natural, warm geothermal pool.

  • The minimum age for this tour is 8 years, and the minimum shoe size to be able to participate in the tour is EU 34

  • Food & Refreshments: We will stop at locations such as gas stations and small shops where you can purchase snacks, sandwiches, and drinks. We recommend bringing a reusable water bottle and any personal snacks you may need for the day.

Meeting & Pickup Details:

  • Pickup starts before departure and may take some time depending on traffic and your location.
  • Please be ready at your chosen pickup location on time, as we follow a strict schedule.
  • If you are staying outside the designated pickup zones, we recommend meeting at a central pickup point in Reykjavík.
  • Pickup outside of the Reykjavík capital area is not included in the package unless it is on the way.
  • A full list of available pickup locations will be provided during the booking process. If you have any questions, please contact us in advance.

  • All day tours can be cancelled up to 24 hours from the departure time by sending a written email to info@troll.is. You will receive a full refund except the 3% card fee. All changes and cancellations requested less than 24 hours from the departure time are non refundable.
  • In the case of cancellation of the tour by the operator because of extreme weather, road conditions or other factors, we will assist you with re-booking to another date or booking other activities when possible, or provide a full refund.
  • Our Landmannalaugar tour requires a minimum of 6 participants to operate. In the rare event that this minimum is not met, we may have to cancel the tour or ask you to move your booking to an alternative date.
